#11–2 hours · MarketMercado Municipal

São Paulo's historic central market, opened in 1933, occupies a grand art-deco building in the Zona Cerealista.

#22–4 hours · MuseumMASP (Museu de Arte de São Paulo)

Modern art museum in a striking 1968 cantilevered building by Lina Bo Bardi overlooking Avenida Paulista.

#31–2 hours · MuseumPinacoteca do Estado

Brazil's oldest art museum, opened in 1905, specialises in 19th and 20th-century Brazilian painting and sculpture.

45–60 min · Historic BuildingTheatro Municipal

A Beaux-Arts masterpiece completed in 1911, modelled on the Paris Opéra.

30–45 min · Railway StationEstação da Luz

Ornate Victorian railway station completed in 1901, designed by British engineers.
