Japan's largest art museum, housing over 110,000 objects spanning Japanese sculpture, painting, ceramics, and samurai armour.

#32–3 hours · ParkUeno Park

A 53-hectare public park in northeast Tokyo, home to museums, temples, a zoo, and a pond.

45–60 min · NeighborhoodAsakusa Street

The historic pedestrian lane leading to Senso-ji Temple, lined with traditional wooden shops selling souvenirs, snacks, and crafts.

30–60 min · Street MarketNakamise Shopping Street

A 250-meter pedestrian alley between Senso-ji's main gate and temple entrance, lined with roughly 90 small shops selling traditional souvenirs, snacks, and crafts.

20–30 min · ShrineAsakusa Shrine

A small Shinto shrine built in 1649 to honor the fishermen who discovered the Senso-ji temple statue.

30–45 min · MuseumAsakusa Culture and Tourism Center

A modern building offering free access to exhibits on Asakusa's history, traditional crafts, and seasonal festivals.

60–90 min · ParkAsakusa Hanayashiki

Japan's oldest amusement park, operating since 1853.
