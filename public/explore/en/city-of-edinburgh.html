#12–3 hours · CastleEdinburgh Castle

Fortress perched on an extinct volcanic rock overlooking the city, home to Scottish royal regalia and centuries of military history.

#21–2 hours · StreetRoyal Mile

Historic cobbled street descending from Edinburgh Castle to Holyrood Palace, lined with centuries-old buildings, shops, cafés, and museums.

#345–75 min · ViewpointCalton Hill

Hilltop landmark crowned with neoclassical monuments, including the National Monument (modelled on the Parthenon) and the Nelson Monument.

2–3 hours · MuseumNational Museum of Scotland

Five-storey institution covering Scottish natural history, art, design, and culture from prehistoric times to the present.

1–2 hours · PalacePalace of Holyroodhouse

The official Scottish residence of the monarch, standing at the eastern foot of the Royal Mile.

45–60 min · CemeteryGreyfriars Kirkyard

Historic graveyard dating to 1562, surrounding Greyfriars Kirk church.

1–2 hours · MuseumScottish National Gallery

Art museum housing Scottish and European paintings from the 14th century onwards, including works by Raeburn, Ramsay, and Turner.

1–1.5 hours · BuildingScottish Parliament Building

Modern parliament building opened in 2004, designed by Catalan architect Enric Miralles.
