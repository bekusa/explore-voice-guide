#130–45 min · SquarePlace de la Comédie

The grand civic heart of Montpellier, ringed by neoclassical buildings and the ornate Opéra Comédie.

#21.5–2.5 hours · MuseumMusée Fabre

One of France's finest art museums, housing European paintings from the 14th to 19th centuries.

#345–60 min · ViewpointPromenade du Peyrou

A broad elevated terrace and gardens built in the 17th century, commanding views across Montpellier to the Cévennes mountains.

40–50 min · NeighborhoodQuartier Antigone

A postmodern residential and commercial quarter designed by Ricardo Bofill in the 1980s, featuring pastel-coloured buildings, columned arcades, and geometric plazas.
