#11–2 hours · PalaceReichstag Building

Germany's parliament building, completed in 1894, stands as a symbol of democracy and national history.

#220–30 min · MonumentBrandenburg Gate

An 18th-century neoclassical triumphal arch and Berlin's most recognisable symbol.

#32–3 hours · MuseumPergamon Museum

Houses monumental architectural treasures including the Pergamon Altar and Ishtar Gate.

1.5–2.5 hours · MuseumNeues Museum

Rebuilt after World War II, this museum holds Egyptian antiquities and the Bust of Nefertiti.

1–1.5 hours · MonumentMemorial to the Murdered Jews of Europe

A 19,000-square-metre field of 2,711 concrete slabs of varying heights, designed by Peter Eisenman.

4–6 hours · NeighborhoodMuseum Island

UNESCO World Heritage site comprising five major museums arranged on a compact island in the Spree River.

1–1.5 hours · StreetUnter den Linden

Berlin's grand boulevard stretches 1.4 km from Brandenburg Gate to the Spree, lined with 18th-century palaces, museums, and universities.

1–1.5 hours · CathedralBerlin Cathedral

This early 20th-century Italian Renaissance–style cathedral dominates Museum Island's skyline.

1–1.5 hours · CathedralBerliner Dom

Monumental Protestant cathedral completed in 1905, with an ornate copper dome dominating Museum Island's skyline.

1–2 hours · StreetEast Side Gallery

A 1.3-kilometre stretch of the original Berlin Wall transformed into an open-air gallery.

2–3 hours · PalaceCharlottenburg Palace

Berlin's largest palace, built in the 17th century as a summer residence for Prussian royalty.

1–1.5 hours · MuseumOld National Gallery

A neoclassical temple on Museum Island housing 19th-century German and European art, from Romanticism through Impressionism to early Modernism.

1–3 hours · ParkTiergarten

Berlin's largest urban park, sprawling across 520 hectares of woodland, meadows, and waterways.

30–45 min · PalaceBelvedere Palace

A small rococo pavilion within Charlottenburg's grounds, built as a tea house and belvedere in the 18th century.

30–45 min · MonumentCheckpoint Charlie

The most famous Cold War crossing point between East and West Berlin.

1–2 hours · SquarePotsdamer Platz

A reconstructed urban plaza that once divided East and West Berlin.
