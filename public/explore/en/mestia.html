Cluster of tower-studded hamlets at over 2,000 meters, among the highest continuously inhabited places in Europe and a UNESCO treasure of Upper Svaneti.

The historic core of Mestia features dozens of distinctive defensive stone towers rising 60–70 feet above narrow lanes.

#345–60 min · MuseumSvaneti Museum of History and Ethnography

Housed in a restored traditional Svan house, this museum displays authentic textiles, weapons, and domestic artifacts that document the region's distinct cultural heritage.
