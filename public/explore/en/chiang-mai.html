#11–2 hours · TempleWat Phra That Doi Suthep

Golden-spired temple perched 1,080 metres above the city on Doi Suthep mountain.

#22–3 hours · NeighborhoodOld City

The historic heart of Chiang Mai, enclosed by a moat and remnants of ancient walls.

#345–60 min · TempleWat Chedi Luang

Massive ruined chedi (stupa) dating to the 15th century, once standing 80 metres tall.

45–60 min · TempleWat Phra Singh

Serene temple in the Old City dating to the 14th century, known for its elegant Lanna architecture and intricate wooden details.

1–1.5 hours · Food MarketWarorot Market

Sprawling covered market in the Old City selling fresh produce, flowers, spices, textiles, and local handicrafts.

45–60 min · TempleWat Ched Yod

A 15th-century temple renowned for its distinctive seven-tiered chedi design and ornate stucco details.

45–60 min · TempleWat Chiang Man

The oldest temple in Chiang Mai, founded in 1296 when the city was established.

1–1.5 hours · TempleWat Umong

Unique forest temple on the western outskirts, built in the 1300s with a distinctive square chedi and tunnels carved into the hillside.

2–4 hours · ParkDoi Suthep-Pui National Park

Mountain park covering over 260 square kilometres, encompassing Doi Suthep and Doi Pui peaks.

15–30 min · MonumentThree Kings Monument

A bronze statue in a central plaza honouring the three legendary kings who founded Chiang Mai in 1296.

1–2 hours · MarketChiang Mai Night Bazaar

Evening open-air market in the east of the city offering handicrafts, clothing, souvenirs, and street food.

1–2 hours · MuseumChiang Mai City Arts and Cultural Centre

Modern museum in the Old City documenting Chiang Mai's history from prehistoric times through the Lanna kingdom to the present.
