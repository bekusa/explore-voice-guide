Historic 1935 copper-domed observatory perched above Los Angeles with free public access to telescopes, planetarium shows, and sweeping city views.

#32–4 hours · MuseumGetty Center

Hilltop art museum opened in 1997, housing Greek and Roman antiquities, medieval manuscripts, European paintings, and contemporary photography.

Major film studio and entertainment complex in the Hollywood Hills featuring movie-themed attractions, live shows, and behind-the-scenes studio tours.

Sidewalk embedded with over 2,700 brass stars honouring entertainment figures, stretching along Hollywood Boulevard and Vine Street since 1960.
