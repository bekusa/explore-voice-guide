#11–2 hours · SkyscraperBurj Khalifa

The world's tallest building at 828 metres, completed in 2010.

#21–2 hours · Cultural CentreSheikh Mohammed Centre for Cultural Understanding

A modest building in Al Fahidi neighbourhood dedicated to bridging Emirati and international cultures.

#31–2 hours · NeighborhoodAl Fahidi Historical District

Dubai's oldest surviving neighbourhood, with restored traditional houses, windtowers, and narrow lanes dating to the 19th and early 20th centuries.

45–90 min · MuseumDubai Museum

Housed in a 19th-century fort, this museum traces Dubai's transformation from a pearl-diving settlement to a global metropolis.

1–1.5 hours · NeighborhoodBastakiya Quarter

A restored quarter of traditional coral-stone and wooden houses within Al Fahidi, dating to the early 1900s.

1–2 hours · MarketGold Souk

A sprawling traditional marketplace in Deira filled with hundreds of gold jewellery shops.

1–1.5 hours · MosqueJumeirah Mosque

A striking modern mosque with white-marble domes and minarets, completed in 1998.

45–90 min · MarketDeira Spice Souk

A historic spice market in Deira's old trading district, filled with stalls selling cardamom, saffron, frankincense, dried limes, and local herbs.

20–40 min · MonumentDubai Fountain

A choreographed fountain system on Burj Khalifa Lake, shooting water up to 150 metres high in synchronized sequences set to music.

30–60 min · WaterwayDubai Creek

A natural inlet that historically connected Dubai to the Gulf and enabled the pearl-diving and trading economy.
