📍 About this place

Labyrinthine quarter on the left bank of the Mtkvari, with narrow winding streets, traditional wooden balconies, and buildings dating from the 6th to 19th centuries. The district preserves the medieval street plan and remains the cultural heart of Tbilisi.

🎒 Practical tips

Wander the side streets early morning before tour groups arrive. The smell of fresh bread from family bakeries and the sound of water from hidden courtyards create an intimate atmosphere.
