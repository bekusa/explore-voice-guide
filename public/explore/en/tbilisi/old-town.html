Old Town

41.6913° N · 44.8045° E · Neighborhood

Old Town is a neighborhood in Tbilisi, Georgia. Historic quarter on the right bank of the Mtkvari, with narrow winding streets, wooden balconies, and low-rise traditional architecture. Most visitors spend 1–2 hours here.
