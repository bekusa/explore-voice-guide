#12–3 hours · MausoleumTaj Mahal

A white marble mausoleum built in the 17th century by Mughal emperor Shah Jahan for his wife Mumtaz Mahal.

#22–3 hours · FortAgra Fort

A massive red-sandstone fortress built by Akbar in 1565, serving as the seat of Mughal power for over a century.

#33–4 hours · Palace ComplexFatehpur Sikri

An abandoned imperial city built by Akbar in the 16th century, featuring red sandstone palaces, courtyards, and a mosque.

1–1.5 hours · MausoleumItimad-ud-Daulah

A marble mausoleum built in the early 17th century for the father of Empress Nur Jahan.

1–1.5 hours · GardenMehtab Bagh

A Mughal garden on the opposite bank of the Yamuna River, offering a framed view of the Taj Mahal from across the water.

1–1.5 hours · MausoleumAkbar's Tomb

Located in Sikandra, a suburb of Agra, this red sandstone mausoleum was begun by Akbar himself and completed by his son Jahangir.

30–45 min · MausoleumChini-ka-Rauza

A 17th-century tomb of Shah Jahan's prime minister, notable for its glazed tile work and Persian-style architecture.
