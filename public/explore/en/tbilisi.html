#130–45 min · CathedralMetekhi Church

Medieval church in central Tbilisi, rebuilt in the 1980s after Soviet demolition.

#21–2 hours · FortressNariqala Fortress

Medieval fortress overlooking Tbilisi's Old Town, built in the 4th century and rebuilt multiple times through Georgian, Persian, and Ottoman rule.

#32–3 hours · MonasteryJvari Monastery

6th-century monastery perched on a hilltop in Mtskheta, 25 km north of Tbilisi.

45–60 min · FortressNarikala Fortress

Ancient citadel overlooking Tbilisi from the east bank of the Mtkvari River.

1–2 hours · NeighborhoodOld Town

Historic quarter on the right bank of the Mtkvari, with narrow winding streets, wooden balconies, and low-rise traditional architecture.

45–90 min · ChurchGergeti Trinity Church

Fifth-century church perched on a hilltop in the Vake district, visible across much of Tbilisi.

20–30 min · SquareLiberty Square

Tbilisi's main plaza, anchored by a towering golden statue of Georgia (Kartlis Deda) and ringed by government buildings and cafés.

3–4 hours · NeighborhoodMtskheta

Ancient capital of Georgia, 20 km north of Tbilisi, where Christianity was established in the 4th century.

25–40 min · CathedralSioni Cathedral

12th-century cathedral in the Old Town, one of Tbilisi's oldest churches.

2–3 hours · TheatreTbilisi State Opera and Ballet Theatre

A neoclassical masterpiece built in 1896, this opera house stands as one of the Caucasus's finest cultural institutions.

4–6 hours · MonasteryDavid Gareja Monastery Complex

A sprawling monastic complex carved into red sandstone cliffs 70 km southeast of Tbilisi, dating to the 6th century.

45–60 min · CathedralSameba Cathedral

Modern white limestone cathedral completed in 2004, dominating the skyline on the north bank of the Mtkvari.

1–2 hours · NeighborhoodAbanotubani

Historic bathing quarter below Narikala Fortress, known since medieval times for its natural sulphur springs.

1–3 hours · MuseumGeorgian National Museum

Large state museum with collections spanning Georgia's archaeology, medieval art, and ethnography.

20–30 min · BasilicaAnchiskhati Basilica

Sixth-century basilica in the Old Town, among the oldest churches in Tbilisi.

15–20 min · BridgePeace Bridge

Modern cable-stayed bridge spanning the Mtkvari River, completed in 2010.

20–30 min · StreetShardeni Street

Pedestrian shopping street connecting Metekhi Square to Freedom Square.

30–40 min · StreetLeselidze Street

Narrow medieval lane in the Old Town, lined with traditional wooden-balconied houses, small galleries, and antique shops.

1–2 hours · ParkTbilisi Botanical Garden

Sprawling across a steep ravine on the city's eastern edge, this garden showcases Georgian flora and exotic plants arranged by region and climate zone.

1–1.5 hours · NeighborhoodVake District

Modern residential and commercial district west of the city center, with wide tree-lined streets, cafés, galleries, and boutique shops.

1–1.5 hours · MuseumGino Chitadze Art Center

Contemporary art museum in a converted 19th-century mansion in Vake, showcasing Georgian and international modern works.

M30–60 min · ParkMtkvari River Waterfront

Riverside promenade and green spaces along the Mtkvari river, with walking paths, benches, and views toward the old town and fortress.

30–45 min · MarketShardeni Street Market

A lively open-air market in central Tbilisi selling fresh produce, spices, dried fruits, and Georgian specialities.

20–30 min · BakeryGino Bread Factory

A working bakery and café in the Vake district famous for its wood-fired traditional Georgian bread and pastries.

30–60 min · ParkVake Park

A central urban park in the Vake neighbourhood featuring tree-lined paths, open lawns, and a small lake.
