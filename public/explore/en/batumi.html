#11–2 hours · PromenadeBatumi Boulevard

A seven-kilometer seaside promenade stretching along the Black Sea coast, lined with palm trees, sculptures, and cafés.

#215–30 min · SculptureAli and Nino Statue

Two eight-meter moving steel figures that merge and pass through each other in a seven-minute cycle, representing the tragic love story from Kurban Said's novel.

#330–60 min · TowerAlphabetic Tower

A 130-meter tower shaped like a DNA helix, its structure decorated with Georgian script letters.

20–40 min · SquarePiazza Square

A small Italian-style plaza in the old town, surrounded by buildings with mosaic facades, stained glass, and ornamental balconies.
