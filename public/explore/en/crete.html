#12–4 hours · BeachElafonissi Beach

A shallow lagoon beach on Crete's southwestern tip, famous for its pink-tinged sand created by crushed seashells.

#25–7 hours · CanyonSamaria Gorge

Europe's longest gorge cuts 16 kilometers through the White Mountains of Crete, dropping from 1,250 meters to sea level.

#32–3 hours · Historic FortSpinalonga Fortress

A Venetian fortress island in the Gulf of Elounda, Crete, built in 1579 to defend against Ottoman raids.

2–4 hours · BeachBalos Lagoon

A shallow lagoon where three seas meet at the tip of the Gramvousa peninsula, northwest Crete.

1–2 hours · Archaeological SitePalace of Phaistos

Second-largest Minoan palace complex on Crete, built around 1900 BCE on a hilltop commanding views of the Messara Plain and Mount Ida.

1–2 hours · Ancient RuinsGortyn Archaeological Site

Sprawling Roman provincial capital across olive groves, preserving the monumental Odeion with Europe's oldest law code inscribed in stone, a 6th-century basilica, and…

1–2 hours · MonasteryArkadi Monastery

Fortified 16th-century monastery 23 kilometers southeast of Rethymno, a symbol of Cretan resistance against Ottoman rule.

1–2 hours · MuseumHeraklion Archaeological Museum

The world's most comprehensive collection of Minoan artefacts, spanning 5,500 years.

1–2 hours · Mountain VillageKritsa Village

Traditional Cretan village climbing the slopes above Agios Nikolaos, known for handwoven textiles and narrow stone lanes.

2–4 hours · BeachAgia Triada Beach

A sheltered sandy cove on Crete's northern coast near Chania, framed by rocky headlands and shallow turquoise water.

1–2 hours · Archaeological SiteKnossos Palace

The largest Bronze Age site on Crete, believed to be the ceremonial centre of Minoan civilisation.

Full day (multi–site) · Walking RouteMinoan Palaces Trail

A self-guided circuit connecting Crete's three major Minoan palace sites—Knossos, Phaistos, and Malia—across the central and southern plains.

45–75 min · Archaeological SiteLato Archaeological Site

Ruins of a Dorian city perched on a ridge near Kritsa, dating to the 7th century BCE.

1–2 hours · BeachMatala Beach

A sandy bay backed by cliffs honeycombed with Roman-era tombs carved into the sandstone.

20–30 min · MuseumSamaria Gorge National Park Visitor Centre

Located at Xyloskalo (the gorge's northern entrance), this small facility explains the geology, flora, and endemic species of the gorge.
