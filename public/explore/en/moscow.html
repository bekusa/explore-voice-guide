#245–75 min · CathedralSaint Basil's Cathedral

Built 1555–1561 for Ivan the Terrible, this UNESCO-listed cathedral is instantly recognizable by its nine colorful onion domes.

#32–3 hours · FortressKremlin

The fortified heart of Moscow, dating to the 12th century.

2–3 hours · MuseumTretyakov Gallery

Russia's premier museum of Russian art, spanning from medieval icons to contemporary works.

1.5–2 hours · MuseumArmoury Chamber

This Kremlin museum houses one of the world's finest collections of Russian royal regalia, armour, thrones, and ceremonial objects spanning five centuries.

60–90 min · CathedralChrist the Saviour Cathedral

A massive Russian Orthodox cathedral completed in 1997 on the banks of the Moskva River, rebuilt after Stalin's original 1883 structure was demolished in 1931.

Founded in 1872, this museum occupies a distinctive red-brick building on Red Square's north side.

30–45 min · MarketGUM Department Store

A three-story arcade of shops and restaurants built in 1893, facing Red Square.
