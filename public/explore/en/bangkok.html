#12–3 hours · PalaceGrand Palace

Sprawling 18th-century royal complex housing Wat Phra Kaew and the Emerald Buddha, Thailand's most sacred image.

#245–90 min · TempleWat Arun

Riverside temple rising 70 metres in ornate Khmer-style spires, covered in millions of coloured porcelain fragments from Chinese ships.

#31–2 hours · TempleWat Mahathat

Sprawling 14th-century royal temple at Ayutthaya's heart, famous for the Buddha head entwined in tree roots.

1–2 hours · TempleWat Pho

Temple complex housing the 46-metre Reclining Buddha covered in gold leaf, with mother-of-pearl inlaid feet depicting 108 auspicious symbols.

1–2 hours · WaterwayChao Phraya River

Bangkok's lifeline flows through the city for 32 kilometres, lined with temples, palaces, markets, and traditional wooden houses (shophouses).

1.5–2 hours · NeighborhoodChinatown (Yaowarat)

Bangkok's historic Chinese quarter pulses with narrow alleys, gold shops, herbal medicine stalls, and restaurants serving Teochew and Cantonese food.

1–2 hours · MuseumJim Thompson House

The teak wood home of American silk entrepreneur Jim Thompson, who revived Thailand's silk industry after World War II before mysteriously disappearing in Malaysia in 1967.

2–4 hours · MarketChatuchak Weekend Market

One of the world's largest weekend markets, sprawling across 35 acres with over 8,000 stalls selling everything from antiques and ceramics to clothing, plants, and street…

2–3 hours · PalaceVimanmek Mansion

The world's largest golden teak wood building, constructed in 1868 as a royal residence.

1–1.5 hours · TempleWat Benchamabophit

The 'Marble Temple' built in 1911 with Italian white Carrara marble, combining Thai and European design.

1–2 hours · ParkLumphini Park

Bangkok's oldest public park, a 142-acre green space created in the 1920s by King Rama VI.

30–45 min · TempleWat Mangkon Kamalawat

Bangkok's oldest and largest Chinese Buddhist temple, built in 1871.

45–60 min · TempleWat Saket

Known as the Golden Mount, this temple sits atop an artificial hill built from the earth dredged during canal construction in the 19th century.

45–90 min · TempleWat Traimit

Home to the Golden Buddha, a five-ton solid-gold seated Buddha statue cast in the 13th century.

2–3 hours · MuseumBangkok National Museum

Southeast Asia's largest museum, housed in an 18th-century palace complex near the Grand Palace.

2–3 hours · MarketDamnoen Saduak Floating Market

A historic floating market 100 km southwest of Bangkok, where vendors sell produce, flowers, and prepared food from wooden boats on narrow canals.

1–2 hours · MarketKhlong Lat Mayom Floating Market

A working canal market in northwest Bangkok where vendors still paddle wooden boats selling fresh produce, prepared food, and flowers.

60–90 min · MuseumSuan Pakkad Palace

A compound of eight traditional Thai teak houses set in a lush garden, housing the private art collection of a Bangkok noble family.

1–2 hours · NeighborhoodTalad Noi

A historic riverside neighborhood of wooden shophouses, narrow alleys, and family-run shops in old Yaowarat.

1–2 hours · MarketTaling Chan Floating Market

A working floating market on a narrow canal west of the city, where vendors sell fresh produce, cooked food, and souvenirs from wooden boats.

40–50 min · TempleWat Suthat

A 19th-century temple with a towering 41-metre central chedi and ornate interior murals depicting Buddhist scenes.

1–2 hours · MuseumErawan Museum

Art museum housed in a three-storey teak mansion near Lumpini.

1–2 hours · StreetSukhumvit Road

Bangkok's longest road stretches 56 kilometres through the city's modern heart, lined with shopping malls, hotels, restaurants, and the BTS Skytrain.

45–60 min · TempleWat Prayun

A riverside temple with an unusual artificial hill inside its grounds, topped by a chedi and surrounded by caves containing Buddha statues.

30–45 min · TempleWat Ratchanatdaram (Loha Prasat)

An unusual metal-roofed temple with 37 spires arranged in a square, built in 1846.

45–90 min · SquareSanam Luang

Large ceremonial field north of the Grand Palace, historically used for royal events and public gatherings.

40–60 min · TempleWat Chaloem Phra Kiat Worawihan

A modern temple in Thonburi with striking architecture and a towering chedi.

1–3 hours · Shopping ComplexSiam Paragon

A sprawling luxury mall in central Bangkok housing international brands, Thai boutiques, and a high-end aquarium.

20–40 min · TempleWat Salas Loi

A small, rarely-visited temple on the Chao Phraya's east bank, known for its peaceful riverside setting and ornate wooden structures.

1–2 hours · NeighborhoodSamsen Road

A historic residential and commercial street in old Bangkok, north of the Grand Palace.
