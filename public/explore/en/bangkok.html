Grand Palace2–3 hours

Sprawling 18th-century royal complex housing Wat Phra Kaew and the Emerald Buddha, Thailand's most sacred image.

Palace Wat Arun45–90 min

Riverside temple rising 70 metres in ornate Khmer-style spires, covered in millions of coloured porcelain fragments from Chinese ships.

Temple Wat Mahathat1–2 hours

Sprawling 14th-century royal temple at Ayutthaya's heart, famous for the Buddha head entwined in tree roots.

Temple Wat Pho1–2 hours

Temple complex housing the 46-metre Reclining Buddha covered in gold leaf, with mother-of-pearl inlaid feet depicting 108 auspicious symbols.

Temple Chao Phraya River1–2 hours

Bangkok's lifeline flows through the city for 32 kilometres, lined with temples, palaces, markets, and traditional wooden houses (shophouses).

Bangkok's historic Chinese quarter pulses with narrow alleys, gold shops, herbal medicine stalls, and restaurants serving Teochew and Cantonese food.

Neighborhood Jim Thompson House1–2 hours

The teak wood home of American silk entrepreneur Jim Thompson, who revived Thailand's silk industry after World War II before mysteriously disappearing in Malaysia in 1967.
