Louvre Museum

Louvre Museum is the world's largest and most-visited art museum, set in a former royal palace beside the Seine in Paris, France. Plan 2–4 hours for the highlights — the Mona Lisa, the Venus de Milo, the Winged Victory. The free Lokali audio guide tells its story in 45 languages, offline, at no cost.

Time needed: 2–4 hours (highlights) · Métro: Palais-Royal – Musée du Louvre (1, 7) · GPS: 48.8606, 2.3352

Masterpieces and where to find them

  1. Mona Lisa — Leonardo's portrait of Lisa Gherardini, whose shifting expression made it the most famous painting in the world. Denon Wing, Level 1, Salle 711
  2. The Wedding Feast at Cana — Veronese's vast Venetian banquet hangs directly opposite the Mona Lisa — the largest painting in the Louvre. Denon Wing, Level 1, Salle 711
  3. Venus de Milo — The Hellenistic marble Aphrodite found on Milos in 1820, famous precisely for her missing arms. Sully Wing, Level 0, Salle 345
  4. Winged Victory of Samothrace — A winged Nike landing on a ship's prow at the top of the Daru staircase, her drapery pressed flat by sea wind. Denon Wing, Level 1, Salle 703
  5. The Raft of the Medusa — Géricault's monumental scene of a real 1816 shipwreck scandal, painted from corpses he studied in morgues. Denon Wing, Level 1, Salle 700
  6. The Coronation of Napoleon — David's wall-sized ceremony in which Napoleon crowns Josephine himself, before a court he partly invented. Denon Wing, Level 1, Salle 702
  7. Liberty Leading the People — Delacroix's Liberty carries the tricolour over the barricades of the July Revolution of 1830. Denon Wing, Level 1, Salle 700
  8. Psyche Revived by Cupid's Kiss — Canova's marble holds the instant just before a kiss, polished until stone looks like skin. Denon Wing, Level 0, Salle 403
  9. The Lacemaker — Vermeer's smallest and most concentrated painting: a young woman bent over her lace pillow. Richelieu Wing, Level 2, Salle 837
  10. Seated Scribe — A painted limestone official from ancient Egypt whose inlaid crystal eyes still look alive after 4,500 years. Sully Wing, Level 1, Salle 635
  11. Great Sphinx of Tanis — Nearly five metres of granite lion with a pharaoh's head, guarding the entrance to the Egyptian galleries. Sully Wing, Level -1, Salle 338
  12. Code of Hammurabi — The basalt stele carrying Babylon's laws in cuneiform — one of the oldest legal texts on earth. Richelieu Wing, Level 0, Salle 227

Common questions

How long do you need at the Louvre?

Plan 2–4 hours for the highlights. First-time visitors usually want at least 3 hours; seeing everything would take days, so pick a few wings and walk slowly. The free 8-minute Lokali audio guide gives you the story as you go.

What should you not miss at the Louvre?

Start with the Mona Lisa (Denon Wing, Salle 711), the Winged Victory at the top of the Daru staircase and the Venus de Milo. Then pick from the table on this page — every work lists its wing and room, so you can build your own route in minutes.

How is Lokali different from the museum's own audio guide?

The museum rents an audio device on site in 9 languages. Lokali runs free on your own phone in 45 languages, works offline once downloaded, and you can listen before you even arrive.
