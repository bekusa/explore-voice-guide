#12–4 hours · BeachPlaya Norte

The island's most famous stretch of sand on the northern shore, with calm turquoise waters, soft white sand, and a relaxed beach-bar atmosphere.

#22–3 hours · Nature ReservePunta Sur Eco Park

The southern tip of the island protects mangrove forests, coral reefs, and a lighthouse with 360-degree views of the Caribbean and Laguna Makax.

#31–1.5 hours · Wildlife CenterTurtle Farm (Granja de Tortugas)

A marine-turtle rescue and breeding facility where visitors observe hatchlings, juveniles, and adults in various pools.

45–60 min · Historical SiteMundaca Fortress

The stone ruins of a 19th-century hacienda and fortress built by pirate-turned-settler Fermín Mundaca on the island's western shore.
