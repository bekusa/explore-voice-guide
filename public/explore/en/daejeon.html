#21–3 hours · NeighborhoodYuseong Hot Spring District

A wellness district centered on natural hot springs, with numerous bathhouses (jjimjilbangs), spas, and traditional Korean bath culture.

#31–2 hours · ParkHanbat Arboretum

A 82-hectare botanical garden featuring over 2,500 plant species arranged in themed zones.

1–2 hours · ViewpointGyejoksan Mountain

A modest 210-metre peak on Daejeon's eastern edge offering hiking trails and a summit viewpoint overlooking the city and surrounding valleys.
