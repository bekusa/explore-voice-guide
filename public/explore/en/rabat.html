#11–2 hours · FortressKasbah of the Udayas

A 12th-century fortified settlement perched above the Bou Regreg River, featuring blue-and-white washed streets, traditional riads, and sweeping views over the Atlantic.

#230–45 min · MausoleumMausoleum of Muhammad V

A stunning modernist-Islamic monument completed in 1975, housing the tomb of Morocco's independence leader.

#330–45 min · MinaretHassan Tower

An unfinished 12th-century minaret begun under the Almohad dynasty, standing 44 metres tall with intricate geometric stone carving.

1–1.5 hours · RuinsChellah

An archaeological site south of the medina, featuring Roman-era remains (the ancient city of Sala Colonia) overlaid with medieval Islamic structures.
