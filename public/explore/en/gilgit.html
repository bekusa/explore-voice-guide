#12–3 days · MeadowFairy Meadows

A high-altitude alpine meadow at 3,600 metres, accessible from Gilgit via Nanga Parbat base camp.

#28–10 hours · Mountain PassShandur Top

A 3,734-metre pass connecting Gilgit to Chitral, crossing the Hindu Kush range.

#32–4 hours · ValleyNaltar Valley

A lush side valley east of Gilgit, known for pine forests, freshwater streams, and seasonal wildflowers.

1–2 hours · MarketGilgit Bazaar

The historic central market of Gilgit, selling traditional crafts, spices, dried fruits, and textiles.

1–2 hours · Rock CarvingKargah Buddha

A 7th-century rock carving of Buddha in a side valley south of Gilgit, one of the few surviving Buddhist monuments in the region.
