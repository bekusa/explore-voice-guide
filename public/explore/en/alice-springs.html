#12–3 hours · Wildlife ParkAlice Springs Desert Park

A 1.2-hectare open-air zoo showcasing native Australian desert fauna—red kangaroos, dingoes, bearded dragons, and over 150 bird species—in naturalistic enclosures.

#21–2 hours · MuseumAlice Springs Telegraph Station

The 1872 telegraph relay station that sparked Alice Springs' founding.

#31–1.5 hours · MuseumRoyal Flying Doctor Service Visitor Centre

An exhibition documenting the Royal Flying Doctor Service, which provides emergency medical care across remote Australia.

1–2 hours · NeighborhoodTodd River Dry Bed

The usually dry bed of the Todd River running through Alice Springs town centre, framed by red banks and river red gums.
