#160–90 min · Historic StreetSanmachi Suji

Three beautifully preserved merchant streets from the Edo period, lined with wooden machiya townhouses, sake breweries, and small museums.

#245–60 min · Historic BuildingTakayama Jinya

A rare surviving samurai-era government administrative building from 1692, now a museum.

#335–45 min · MuseumTakayama Matsuri Yatai Kaikan

Houses four of the eleven ornate festival floats (yatai) used in Takayama's twice-yearly matsuri celebrations.

90–120 min · Open-Air MuseumHida Folk Village

An open-air collection of over 30 traditional farmhouses, workshops, and storehouses relocated from the Hida region.
