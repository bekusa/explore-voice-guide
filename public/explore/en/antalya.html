#11–2 hours · Ancient TheaterAspendos Theatre

The best-preserved Roman theater in the Mediterranean world, built in the second century and still seating thousands for performances.

#22–3 hours · Historic QuarterKaleiçi

Antalya's walled Old Town, a maze of Ottoman-era houses, narrow cobbled lanes, and flower-draped courtyards descending to the Roman harbour.

#345–60 min · WaterfallDüden Waterfall

A dramatic 40-metre waterfall where the Düden River plunges directly into the Mediterranean.

15–20 min · Roman MonumentHadrian's Gate

A triple-arched marble gate built in 130 AD to honour Emperor Hadrian's visit.
