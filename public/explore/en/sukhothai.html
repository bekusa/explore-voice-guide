#130–45 min · TempleWat Si Chum

A 13th-century temple within Sukhothai Historical Park, notable for its massive seated Buddha image (15 metres tall) enclosed within a brick mondop structure.

#21–1.5 hours · TempleWat Saphan Hin

A 13th-century hilltop temple on the northern edge of Sukhothai Historical Park, accessible by a long stone bridge.

#345–60 min · TempleWat Phra Phai Luang

A northern temple complex within Sukhothai Historical Park with three large chedis (stupas) and a large seated Buddha image.

1–1.5 hours · MuseumRamkhamhaeng National Museum

Located in modern Sukhothai town, this museum houses sculptures, ceramics, and artefacts from the Sukhothai period (13th–15th centuries).
