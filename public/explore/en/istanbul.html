Free audio guides in Istanbul

Every place below has a free audio guide in 45 languages

#145–90 min · MosqueBlue Mosque

Built in the early 17th century, this Ottoman mosque is named for the blue İznik tiles that cover its interior walls and dome.

#21–2 hours · MuseumHagia Sophia

Completed in 537 CE, this Byzantine cathedral was the largest domed structure in the world for over a thousand years.

#32–3 hours · PalaceTopkapi Palace

Residence of Ottoman sultans from the 15th to 19th centuries, this sprawling palace complex overlooks the Golden Horn and contains courtyards, gardens, and pavilions.

S30–60 min · SquareSultanahmet Square

This UNESCO World Heritage plaza is bounded by the Blue Mosque, Hagia Sophia, and the Basilica Cistern.

30–45 min · LandmarkBasilica Cistern

An underground Byzantine water reservoir built in the 6th century, this subterranean hall holds 336 columns reflected in shallow water.

1–2 hours · MarketGrand Bazaar

One of the world's oldest and largest covered markets, dating to the 15th century, with over 4,000 shops arranged in vaulted corridors.

I2–3 hours · MuseumIstanbul Archaeology Museums

A complex of three museums in the Topkapı grounds housing artifacts from Mesopotamia, Anatolia, Greece, and Rome.

45–60 min · MosqueSüleymaniye Mosque

Commissioned by Süleyman the Magnificent and completed in 1557, this grand mosque dominates the skyline above the Golden Horn.

30–60 min · ViewpointBosphorus Strait

The narrow waterway separating Europe and Asia, lined with palaces, fortresses, and fishing villages.

2–3 hours · NeighborhoodFener and Balat

Two adjacent historic quarters along the Golden Horn, known for steep cobbled lanes, pastel-painted houses, and layered religious heritage.

1.5–2.5 hours · MuseumIstanbul Archaeological Museums

This three-building complex houses one of the world's richest collections of ancient artefacts, spanning Mesopotamian, Egyptian, Greek, and Anatolian civilizations.

1–2 hours · CastleRumeli Fortress

A 15th-century Ottoman fortress on the European shore of the Bosphorus, built by Mehmed II before the conquest of Constantinople.

1–2 hours · NeighborhoodBalat

Historic neighbourhood on the Golden Horn's western shore, home to Ottoman-era wooden houses painted in pastel yellows, blues, and pinks.

45–60 min · MuseumChora Church

A Byzantine church dating to the 4th century, later expanded and decorated with remarkable mosaics and frescoes during the 14th century.

4–6 hours · IslandPrinces' Islands

A small archipelago in the Sea of Marmara, reached by ferry from the European shore.

30–45 min · MarketSpice Bazaar

Built in the 17th century, this L-shaped market near the Galata Bridge specialises in spices, dried fruits, nuts, and Turkish sweets.

2–3 hours · MuseumRahmi M. Koç Museum

An industrial museum in a restored foundry and shipyard on the Golden Horn.

1–2 hours · BathhouseÇemberlitaş Hamamı

A working Ottoman bathhouse built in 1584 by Mimar Sinan's student Mehmet Ağa.

2–3 hours · NeighborhoodKadıköy

A district on the Asian shore known for its produce market, street food, and residential streets lined with cafés and independent bookshops.

30–45 min · MosqueMihrimah Sultan Mosque

A Mimar Sinan mosque from 1562, built for Süleyman the Magnificent's daughter.

30–60 min · ViewpointPierre Loti Café

A hilltop café in Eyüp overlooking the Golden Horn, named after the French novelist who frequented the area.

45–90 min · ParkGülhane Park

The outer gardens of Topkapı Palace, opened to the public in 1912.
