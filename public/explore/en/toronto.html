#11–2 hours · LandmarkCN Tower

Canada's most recognizable structure, this 553-metre communications tower dominates Toronto's skyline.

#245–90 min · Food MarketSt. Lawrence Market

A historic public market operating since 1803, housed in a Victorian building.

#32–4 hours · ParkCentre Island

The largest and most developed of Toronto's island parks, Centre Island offers beaches, gardens, and recreational facilities.

2–4 hours · MuseumRoyal Ontario Museum

Canada's largest museum, housing natural history, world cultures, and contemporary art across 40 galleries.

2–3 hours · MuseumArt Gallery of Ontario

One of North America's largest art museums, with Canadian, African, contemporary, and European collections spanning medieval to modern.

1–3 hours · NeighborhoodDistillery District

A pedestrian village of Victorian-era industrial buildings converted into galleries, shops, restaurants, and studios.

20–40 min · Historic BuildingGooderham Building

An iconic red-brick Victorian warehouse from 1892, shaped like a flatiron, located at the confluence of two downtown streets.

30–45 min · LandmarkGibraltar Point Lighthouse

Built in 1808, this is Toronto's oldest surviving structure.

1–2 hours · NeighborhoodKensington Market

A bohemian warren of narrow lanes, vintage shops, street art, and food stalls.

2–4 hours · ParkToronto Islands

A chain of small islands in Lake Ontario, connected by ferries and bridges.

1–2 hours · CastleCasa Loma

A Gothic Revival mansion built in 1911 by a wealthy industrialist, perched on a hill overlooking the city.

20–30 min · Street ArtGraffiti Alley

A laneway in the Fashion District where street artists are permitted to paint murals legally.

1–2 hours · MuseumRipley's Aquarium of Canada

A modern aquarium adjacent to the CN Tower, housing over 20,000 marine animals.

1–2 hours · BeachHanlan's Point Beach

Located on the western tip of Toronto Islands, Hanlan's Point is a long sandy beach popular for swimming and windsurfing.

45–90 min · ViewpointScarborough Bluffs

Dramatic 40-metre clay cliffs rising from Lake Ontario's shoreline at the eastern edge of Toronto.

2–3 hours · ParkCentreville Amusement Park

A family-friendly amusement park on Centre Island featuring vintage and modern rides, games, and attractions.
