#130–45 min · BridgeCharles Bridge

A 14th-century stone bridge spanning the Vltava River, lined with Baroque statues and connecting Old Town to Prague Castle.

#22–4 hours · CastlePrague Castle

A sprawling hilltop fortress and the official seat of Czech presidents since the 9th century.

#31–2 hours · CathedralSaint Vitus Cathedral

A Gothic masterpiece begun in the 14th century with soaring ribbed vaults, rose windows, and stained glass by Alfons Mucha.

1–2 hours · NeighborhoodJewish Quarter

A walled medieval district preserving six centuries of Prague's Jewish heritage through synagogues, a cemetery, and museum collections.

1–1.5 hours · MonasteryStrahov Monastery

Founded in 1143, this Premonstratensian monastery sits on a hilltop west of Prague Castle, housing two stunning baroque libraries and a working monastery.

45–60 min · ViewpointPetřín Lookout Tower

An iron lattice tower built in 1891 on a hilltop west of the city centre, modelled after the Eiffel Tower but smaller.

30–40 min · TowerPowder Tower

A 15th-century Gothic tower marking the entrance to Old Town, originally used to store gunpowder.
