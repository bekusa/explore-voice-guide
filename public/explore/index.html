Place of the day

One place, one story

Blue Mosque

Blue Mosque is a mosque in Istanbul, Türkiye. Built in the early 17th century, this Ottoman mosque is named for the blue İznik tiles that cover its interior walls and dome. Most visitors spend 45–90 min here. The free Lokali audio guide runs 9 minutes.

Questions

Good to know

Are Lokali audio guides free?

Yes. Every guide is free: no ticket, no subscription and no tour group. You can listen in the app or in your browser.

Which languages can I listen in?

50+. Pick your language once and every guide plays in it, including the key facts, what to look for and practical tips.

Do the guides work offline?

Yes, in the app. Download a city before you travel and the guides play without mobile data.

How are the guides written?

Each guide is drafted with AI assistance from a fixed research brief, checked against structured place data and cross-referenced with Wikipedia and Wikidata. How we write these guides
